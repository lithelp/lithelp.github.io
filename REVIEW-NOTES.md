# Study guides: points for the author to check

Rules followed in every guide:
- Quotations come only from text already on the LitHelp pages or from the official 2016 paper and marking scheme.
- Questions are labelled either **Official past paper** (with year and question number) or **Practice question · LitHelp**.
- Facts about writers are kept general where I was not certain.

## The Huntsman
- Poem quoted as "its mouth"; the original page has "it's mouth" (looks like a typo).
- Lowbury biography kept general: English poet who was also a doctor and medical scientist.

## Fear
- Mistral facts used: pen name, born in Vicuña, schoolteacher, Nobel Prize 1945 (first Latin American winner), collection *Ternura*. I did not state which collection "Fear" first appeared in.

## Bringing Tony Home
- The author's name is spelt "Abeysekera" on the original page; the guide adds "(also spelt Abeysekara)". No dates given.
- Plot details come only from the original page and the 2016 marking scheme. Please check:
  - Who looked after Tony at Depanama after the move (the guide does not say).
  - The 2016 scheme says "Through his journey from Wijerama, he walked without getting on the bus". The guide describes this as the walk back to Wijerama with Tony. Please confirm the direction.
- Practice questions for this novel are recall questions rather than extracts, because I only have the official 2016 passage and the quotation in the marking scheme.

## The Lahore Attack
- The extract quotes the player's name as "Paranvithana", as on the original page (the usual spelling is Paranavitana).
- Background kept general: "Several players were injured, and Pakistani security personnel were among those killed."
- The soldier is described as "on duty at a checkpoint", following the 2016 marking scheme.
